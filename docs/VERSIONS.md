| v7.9.3 Road rage | 24 Nov | Hit someone's car hard (about 18 km/h or more) and, often, the car stops and the driver gets out shouting. | v7.9.4 Impact physics and bodies | 24 Nov | Hitting a car is a real impulse now: the car you hit takes it where you hit it, pushed away and spun about its middle (hit the back corner and it swings round), sliding with tyres that grip sideways harder than they roll, bouncing off walls; heavier cars move less and a bus barely moves. A hard side hit can roll it over, ending on its wheels, side or roof (wrecks are cleared when you're far away). Shooting people (website only) uses hit zones: a head shot drops them at once (no muscle control, the body folds straight down), the arm, leg or stomach wounds them and they hold it (the other hand clamps the arm, a hand presses the thigh and they limp, both hands on the stomach) until they're shot again; a fatal body shot gives a short flinch with a hand to the wound, then the knees go as tension drains and the body tips over like a pendulum about the feet, legs straightening, arms trailing, a small bounce on the ground. No knockback from bullets; anyone moving carries that speed into the fall (runners trip and slide), and people hit by a car are thrown along its path. With blood allowed (16+ and the admin), about a third of victims lie there struggling to breathe for a few seconds (breaths made in code, chest heaving, slowing down) before going still; shooting them again ends it. Cache myib-v7-9-4. Smoke test 56/56. |
