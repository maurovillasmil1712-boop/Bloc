| v7.3 City arms | 23 Nov | Weapons on foot, website only: pistol 400, SMG 1,800, shotgun 2,600, rifle 4,500 coins and armour 1,200, bought at Bullseye Range (a new place with three shooting lanes and swinging targets). G draws and switches, right mouse aims over the right shoulder, click shoots, R reloads; touch has Weapon and Fire buttons. Shots are camera rays that stop at people, cars, targets, walls or the ground; gunfire scares everyone nearby, drivers floor it, the police hear it from 220 m (shooting someone down is two stars) and at two stars officers shoot back. Blood is off by default: a player confirms they are 16 or older in the range, then the admin allows it in Accounts (`POST /api/admin/gore`); until both, people just fall over and get up. Nobody loses coins. Gunshots are made in code. Server: `ARMS`, `ARMOUR_PRICE`, `f.arms`, `f.armour`, `f.gore`, `a:'arm'`, `a:'armour'`, `a:'gore'`, `arms` in the drive start and the farm. Cache myib-v7-3-0. Smoke test 56/56. |
| v7.4 City streets | 23 Nov | You start on the busiest shopping streets, with the café, clothing shop, garage, gym and Bullseye Range a short walk apart (places moved there). Get out of a moving car (up to about 60 km/h): you roll on the road and the car keeps rolling and slows down instead of stopping dead. Steal cars: walk up to a stopped car and press F (or tap Take this car); the driver always reacts and you always say something back; a third of drivers, every bus driver and every officer hold on to the wheel, and you win by holding or tapping F (or Pull on a phone) before they shove you off. Your own car stays parked where you left it and you can go back to it; a seen theft is one star, a police car two. Motorbikes and buses in traffic (riders drawn in code; buses stop at stops and the driver announces them); a Street Bike to buy (2,200 coins) that leans into turns with you on it. Your own plane (30,000 coins, faster, your colour) in the garage panel. Furniture for your flat (12 pieces, 150 to 2,000 coins) ordered from the desk. Dialogue: 2,003 lines per language (gunfire panic, people hit, officers with guns, the range officer, phone calls, tourists, street vendors, comments on your clothes, bikers, the bus, the airport tower, carjacking on both sides, more pairs and conversations); voices being added. Server: `moto` in `CARS`, `PLANE_PRICE`, `FURN`, `a:'plane'`, `a:'furn'`, `plane` and `furn` in the drive start and the farm. Cache myib-v7-4-0. Smoke test 56/56. |
| v7.5 City missions | 23 Nov | Rosa stands outside the café with missions (a violet beam marks her and each objective): Welcome to Bloc City (150), Borrowed wheels (steal a car, lose the police, bring it to the garage; 250), Range day (side mission, website only; 200), Special delivery (port to your flat in five minutes; 300), Sky tour (four rings over the beach and land; 400). The server pays each once, in order, with believable times (`MISSIONS`, `f.missions`, `missions` in the drive start and end). Police on foot: officers get out of a stopped patrol car and run you down (they grab you to arrest you on foot), shoot back at two stars if you have a weapon out, and can be punched or shot down. A police helicopter circles at three stars with a searchlight at night and rotor sound. Your car's paint reflects the street around it on High and Ultra (a reflection probe). Cache myib-v7-5-0. Smoke test 56/56, mission chain and payout runs. |
| v7.6 City phone | 23 Nov | A phone in the city (P or the Phone button): Missions (progress, and Call Rosa to get the next one from anywhere), Camera (photo mode: the HUD hides, the world pauses, drag and scroll around you, the shutter saves a PNG), Map, Radio and Keys. Garage mods, bought once and switched for free: rear spoiler (400), tinted windows (250), neon underglow in five colours (600), rims in chrome, black, gold or white (300); they go on whichever of your cars you drive (`MODS`, `f.mods`, `f.modsOwn`, `a:'mod'`, `mods` in the drive start). Cache myib-v7-6-0. Smoke test 56/56, mods API run. |
| v7.6.1 City fixes | 24 Nov | Walking into a shop no longer freezes the game: rooms don't add lights any more (every new light made all the shaders rebuild on real graphics cards); a fixed pool of three lights moves into the room you enter, and every room is built and compiled in the background a few seconds after the city starts. Real CC0 textures indoors (plaster, brick and concrete walls, wooden and granite floors). Getting out of a moving car: it glides to a stop instead of rolling on or creeping into reverse. Parked cars along the kerbs can be taken (F; the alarm goes off). The minimap turns with the camera and the arrow shows where you face. A bigger buying spot at the Bullseye Range counter. Cache myib-v7-6-1. Smoke test 56/56. |
| v7.7 City map and crime | 24 Nov | The minimap starts closer and zooms with + and − (keys or the two buttons beside it), pulls out as you speed up and comes in on foot; your zoom is remembered. Drive to: tap or click the big map to drop a waypoint (tap it again to clear it), and a yellow route follows the roads on both maps, with the distance under the minimap, a yellow beam at the spot and an arrow at the screen edge when it's out of view; it clears when you arrive, and mission objectives get a violet route by themselves. Big map: a legend that switches places, missions, jobs, homes, trials and crates on and off (remembered), and the player arrow now points the right way. HUD: a live coin counter, health and armour arcs around the minimap, wanted stars beside it. Hold Tab for a weapon wheel (time slows, ammo shown). Website only: hitting someone hard with a car or shooting them down kills them (they're cleared away later, people nearby scream and run, two stars); blood still needs 16+ and the admin. The barista, shop clerk, mechanic, gym coach and range gunsmith can be punched or shot down: the police are called and that place stays closed until your next drive. 30 new reaction lines (2,033 per language). In the app people still get knocked down and get back up. Cache myib-v7-7-0. Smoke test 56/56. |
| v7.8 City challenges | 24 Nov | Street races: four races with a pink ring at the start (Ensanche Sprint, Downtown Dash, Barrio Run, Pine Hills Climb); stop in the ring for a 3-2-1 countdown against Dani, Nadia and Leo, who follow the roads, brake for corners and stay close. Pass every checkpoint in order; the bar shows position, checkpoint and time. First place pays 300 coins the first time and 60 once a day after (`RACES`, `f.races`, `f.drive.races`, `races` in the drive end, with a minimum time per race). Stunt combos in a car: air time, big jumps, drifts and near misses chain into a multiplier up to ×8, bank after 2.5 s, a crash loses it; your best combo stays on the device (no coins). Getting busted now fails "lose the police" steps. Lite quality for old Intel laptops on Safari (no shadows, short view, fewer people; Auto drops to it when Low still stutters). Drive to and races only route on the main road network. Voices re-encoded at 24 kbps so English and Spanish fit under the 100 MB upload. Cache myib-v7-8-0. Smoke test 56/56, race payout run. |
